iLARS

Patient follow-up

Every day between visits.

After rectal cancer surgery, many patients live with bowel problems for months. With iLARS they log their symptoms in two minutes each evening — and their doctor sees the trend, and who needs attention, on one screen.

  • A two-minute daily check-in on the phone
  • A doctor portal that flags worsening scores and missed check-ins
  • Now growing into Lithuania's national colorectal cancer registry

Dr. Filler

Medical records

Look up at your patient.

A Chrome extension for doctors in Lithuania. It listens to the consultation, reads earlier documents and fills in the visit record. The doctor checks it and saves.

  • Fills in E025 and E027 forms and suggests ICD-10 codes
  • Works inside ELI, Foxus, VARUTIS and ESIS
  • A phone app records ward rounds and sends the text to the computer
Visit drfiller.lt (opens in a new tab)

SimuFlow

Simulation centres

Less planning. More teaching.

A phone app and web dashboard that run a medical simulation centre day to day: who is in, who trained on which simulator, and which classes are planned where.

  • Check in with a tap of the phone; simulator time is logged automatically
  • Class planning that blocks double bookings, with reminders an hour before
  • In daily use at Vilnius University's Medical Simulation Centre since September 2026
